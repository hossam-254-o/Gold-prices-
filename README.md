# 📊 Historical Gold Price Analysis (1979 – 2021)

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard analyzing three decades of historical gold price movements across **10 countries**. This project transforms raw, multi-decade financial data into clear, actionable visual insights to track long-term trends, currency impacts, and price volatility.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview
Gold is a key indicator of global economic stability and inflation. The objective of this project is to:
* Track historical gold price fluctuations from **1979 to 2021**.
* Compare gold valuation and purchasing power across **10 different countries and currencies**.
* Identify major economic spikes, volatility periods, and long-term growth trends.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, interactive visualization, and dashboard design.
* **Power Query (M):** ETL pipeline, data cleaning, unpivoting, and data transformation.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom measures for average price per gram, historical growth, and price volatility metrics.

---

## 🔄 Data Preprocessing & Modeling
To prepare the raw dataset for efficient analysis in Power BI, the following steps were executed in **Power Query**:
1. **Unpivoting Data:** Transformed wide, pivoted country and currency columns into a normalized tabular structure (`Date`, `Country/Currency`, `Price`).
2. **Handling Missing Values:** Cleaned null entries and ensured consistent date formatting across the 42-year timeline.
3. **Standardization:** Added calculated measures to standardize measurements, focusing on **Average Price per Gram** alongside standard troy ounce metrics.

---

## 📈 Key Analytical Highlights & Insights
* **Long-Term Trajectory:** Visualized the steady upward trend of gold prices over 30+ years, highlighting sharp surges during global financial crises.
* **Cross-Country Comparison:** Enabled dynamic filtering across 10 countries to observe how local currency fluctuations impact domestic gold prices compared to global benchmarks.
* **Volatility & Averages:** Evaluated historical price volatility and multi-decade average prices per gram to provide a clear financial overview.
